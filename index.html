Skara Brae

A turn-by-turn companion. Four rounds, three turns each.

Every turn you take is these three things, in this order.

1

Take a card

One Village Card from the current set, resolved fully before you touch a worker.

2

Place workers

All of them, one at a time. Finish each placement before starting the next.

3

Take them back

Every worker returns to your side, ready to go again next turn.

Three turns per round. Four rounds. Then score.

Setup

Tap a number as you finish it.

  1. Player board, slider, two markers

    Slider sits over the second column of the storage area. Furnish Marker and Trade Marker both go in the left-most slot of their tracks — that's space 0.

  2. Lay out your 9 Standard Action Tiles

    Your colour, in order, to the right of the board so the artwork lines up. Unflipped side up — each tile shows two resources on a tan banner along the bottom.

  3. All 4 workers into a reserve

    Off to the side. They are not yours to place yet — you earn one per round.

  4. Main supply

    All resources, Roof Cards and Extra Storage Tiles in a shared pile.

  5. Take 2 Skaill Knives

    Into two separate spaces, left of your slider.

  6. Turn Order Tile

    Stack the Turn Markers randomly on the left space.

    Two players: add one extra marker in an unused colour. It's a neutral player.

    Solo: the Turn Order Tile and all Turn Markers go back in the box.

  7. Shuffle the 80 Village Cards

    Facedown draw pile. If it ever runs out, shuffle the discards into a new one.

  8. Solo goals

    1 Focus Card and 4 Task Cards faceup above your board. Rest to the box.

  9. Pick a Special Action Tile

    Deal 2, keep 1. Place it below your Trade tile, Craft cost showing along the bottom. Playing 1–2, you can deal 3 and keep 1 instead.

    Hunt Boar needs 3 boar nearby. Spin Wool needs the 10 Spindle Whorl Cards. Recruit needs the 3 black workers.

Start of a round

Which round are you on?

Turns taken: Three turns, then the round ends.

Who goes now

The tile has three spaces: left (waiting), top (passed), right (done).

Whoever's marker is on top of the left stack is the active player. Two choices, no waiting around — decide as soon as it's you.

  • Take your turn. Move your marker to the right space, on top of what's there. Then play your turn. The next person becomes active immediately while you're still going.
  • Pass. Move your marker to the top space instead. You'll still take a turn — just after everyone else in the left stack — and you'll be earlier in the order for the next set of cards.

When the left stack empties, work through the top space from the top down. Those players must take a turn now.

Once everyone has gone: move the whole right-hand stack back to the left space keeping its order, discard the last Village Card left in the set, and slide the Turn Order Tile along to the next set. Three sets gone means the round is over.

The neutral player. When the spare marker comes up, move it to the right space and discard the left-most Village Card of the current set. Nothing else happens.

No turn order tile. You draw a shrinking hand each turn.

  • Turn 1 — draw 4 Village Cards, keep 1, discard the rest.
  • Turn 2 — draw 3, keep 1.
  • Turn 3 — draw 2, keep 1.

Deal facedown piles of 4, 3 and 2 at the start of the round so you don't lose count.

Task Cards

At any point in a turn, pay the resources on a Task Card to take that action straight away — as though you'd placed a worker there, even if the tile is occupied. Then flip the card facedown. Each one still faceup at the end costs you 2VP.

Focus Card

An end-game goal. Meet it and flip it facedown. Leave it faceup and it costs 5VP.

Step 1 — take a card

From the left-most set. The Turn Order Tile sits beside it as a reminder.

Roof 2VP

Below your board. Each roof shelters one settler at feeding time, so that settler eats nothing. Roofs you gain from actions instead come from the main supply and are only worth 1VP.

Stone Ball 2VP

Below your board, then take 1 Skaill Knife for every stone ball you own, this one included.

Utensils 2VP

Below your board, then take 1 Hide. Permanently: hearth limit +1, and 1 less midden every round.

Settler 1–2VP each on the Furnish track

Placing a settler

  1. Top harvest — mandatory, in full

    Count your icons in that Environment (including the new settler) and gain that many resources of what the Gather tile shows. Offered two types? Any mix. You can't take fewer.

  2. Bottom effect — optional, in full, once

    Gain a specific resource, swap 1 resource for 2 of another, or harvest from a different Environment. Never repeatable.

Skaill Knives. Any time you count Environment icons, spend knives to count as having one more each — for the harvest, the bottom effect, or both. This is the main reason to hold knives, but they're also 1VP apiece at the end.

Step 2 — place workers

Onto your 10 Action Tiles: nine standard, one special.

  • Each tile holds one small worker and one large worker — so place your large one last, it can squeeze in anywhere.
  • Pay costs and resolve effects top to bottom on the tile.
  • Fully finish one worker before placing the next.
  • You may skip placing a worker, though it's rarely worth it.

Then step 3: pick every worker back up.

Jump to what each tile does

Your storage area

The engine and the trap.

  • One resource per space, always left of the slider.
  • Out of room? Push the slider one column right. That's more space and more midden.
  • Slider already at the far right? You still gain resources — they just wait off the board until space appears.
  • The slider only ever moves back left when you clear midden, and only if you have 3 or more empty spaces. You can't dump resources to make room.
  • Rearrange freely. Discard voluntarily, never.
  • Midden can't be spent, ever. "Any resource" never means midden.
  • Run out of a resource type? An Extra Storage Tile holds 1 token standing for 4, covering 4 spaces.

The two holes in your slider are the price of hoarding: the top one is midden per round, the bottom one is VP lost at the end.

End of round

All three turns done, no cards left in the middle. Three steps, in order.

  1. Rest abilities

    Any order. A flipped Craft tile gives you a free Cook action. Recruit returns your black workers.

  2. Feed the settlers

    1 Food per settler, minus 1 per roof. Paying is optional — but every Food you don't pay costs you a settler of your choice. A roof over their head or a meal in their hands.

  3. Gain midden

    As shown by your slider's top hole, minus 1 per Utensils Card.

What each tile does

Blue lines are what changes once the tile is flipped.

Standard

Furnish

Spend wool and hides to move your Furnish Marker one space right. The cost in portions (1, 2 or 3) is printed above the space you're moving into. One portion = 2 Wool or 1 Hide, and you can mix.

Two portions could be 4 wool, or 2 wool and 1 hide, or 2 hides.

The Furnish track sets how much every settler is worth at the end, plus a bonus per complete set of four different Environments.

Flipped: also produces 1 Barley or 1 Skaill Knife.

Cook

Spend resources to make Food, Bone, Hide and Wool. Total weight of what you cook can't exceed your hearth limit, which starts at 4.

Two shells and a fish weighs 4: you get 3 Food and 1 Bone. A deer plus a rabbit weighs 5 — too much.

Flipped: hearth limit +2. Utensils and the Muster tile also raise it.

Craft

Pay the two resources on the tan banner along the bottom of any one Action Tile to turn that tile over. Workers sitting on it just get put back down, no effect.

Every flipped tile is 2VP at the end, on top of its better side.

Flipped: take a free Cook action at the end of every round. Hearth limit still applies.

Clean

Spend 2 or more different resources from hides, barley, seaweed and wood — only one of each type. Clear that many midden and take 1 Roof Card (worth 1VP).

Clearing one midden means either discarding a midden token, or moving your slider one column left. Mix and match when clearing several. The slider needs 3+ empty spaces to move back.

Flipped: clears one more, and Food counts as one of the resource types.

Trade

Spend 2–6 of a single resource type — the number above the space you're moving into — to advance your Trade Marker one space. Any type but midden; food, knives and fish all count.

Straight after, you may buy the resource shown under that space for 1 Skaill Knife.

Flipped: buy with any 1 resource instead, and it needn't match what you spent.

Gather ×4

One tile per Environment. Each worker resolves one of the listed options.

Shore unflipped gives 1 shell or 1 seaweed. Flipped: 1 shell and 1 seaweed, or 1 fish.

Flipped: better options, plus one extra Environment icon of that type whenever you harvest — shown along the very bottom edge. That bonus doesn't change the Gather action itself.

Special — every one costs 2 portions of Food or Bone

2 Food, 2 Bone, or one of each.

Build

Take a Furnish or Trade action for 2 fewer resources, exactly as if you'd placed a worker there.

Flipped: 3 fewer.

Explore

Draw 2 Village Cards, keep 1. A settler kept this way gets no harvest and no bottom effect. Stone balls and utensils still give their immediate resources.

Flipped: draw 3 keep 1, and a settler may resolve its bottom effect.

Hunt Boar

Gain 1 Boar, max 3 at once. The tile shows the boar's weight and what it yields when cooked.

Flipped: also gives 1 Skaill Knife, and boars yield 1 extra hide.

Innovate

Take a Craft action for 2 fewer resources, ignoring the usual cost.

Flipped: spend 1 Food to immediately use the tile you just turned over.

Muster

Resolve either the top harvest or the bottom effect of one Environment, by the settler rules. Owning this tile gives hearth limit +1.

Flipped: resolve both instead of choosing. The bottom effect stays optional.

Recruit

Gain 1 black worker, max 3. They behave like your small workers and go back at the end of the round, ready to hire again.

Flipped: flipped Action Tiles can hold 2 small workers a turn, plus a large one.

Spin Wool

Take a Spindle Whorl Card, place it below your board, then gain 1 Wool per whorl you own including it. Each is 1VP.

Flipped: afterwards you may spend 3 Wool for a Roof Card.

Tend Land

Resolve up to 3 different Gather tiles as if you'd placed workers. Swap any of those for clearing 1 midden instead — discard a midden or move the slider left.

Flipped: up to 4 instead of 3.

Scoring

After round 4. Ties go to whoever's Turn Marker sits higher on the stack.

  • Furnish track — 1 or 2 VP per settler, from the top line under the track. Plus 0, 2, 4 or 6 VP per complete row of four settlers from different Environments, from the bottom line.
  • Trade track — the value under your marker.
  • Flipped tiles — 2VP each.
  • Roofs, utensils, stone balls, whorls — the VP printed on each card.
  • Food and Skaill Knives — 1VP each.
  • Midden — minus 1VP each.
  • Slider — minus whatever shows in its bottom hole.
  • Faceup Focus and Task Cards — minus 5 and minus 2.

Tally it up

Solo target: 45VP is a win, 55VP or more is a strong game. For a real challenge, hit 55 with each of the eight Special Action Tiles in turn.
